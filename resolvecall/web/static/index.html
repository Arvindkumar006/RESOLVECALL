HOW AUTONOMOUS PHONE RECOVERY WORKS

A step-by-step walkthrough of how ResolveCall takes an offline failure and resolves it through an auditable phone conversation.

01

Incident Ingested

WHAT HAPPENS

An operational incident payload arrives via API with failure description, cutoff deadline, and authorized contact phone number.

WHY IT MATTERS

Establishes the immutable baseline constraints and target resolution deadline before any action is attempted.

WHAT RESOLVECALL RECORDS

INCIDENT_RECEIVED event with raw payload, timestamp, and initial status = OPEN.

02

Recovery Plan Formulated

WHAT HAPPENS

The planner parses constraints, extracts access PINs/instructions, and defines negotiation objectives and challenge conditions.

WHY IT MATTERS

Prevents the telephony agent from accepting invalid windows or promising terms outside operational authority.

WHAT RESOLVECALL RECORDS

PLAN_GENERATED with target prompt, objective, and strict validation boundary.

03

CALL-E Telephony Dials

WHAT HAPPENS

CALL-E executes an outbound PSTN call to the authorized vendor phone number and streams real-time connection state.

WHY IT MATTERS

Bypasses missing vendor APIs by meeting the human contact on standard voice telephony.

WHAT RESOLVECALL RECORDS

CALL_INITIATED and CALL_RUNNING with unique CALL-E Run ID and PSTN timestamps.

04

Agent Negotiates Live

WHAT HAPPENS

The agent explains the shipment emergency, states authorized codes, requests an expedited delivery window, and gathers dispatcher name.

WHY IT MATTERS

Handles real human interruptions, clarifications, and counter-offers gracefully.

WHAT RESOLVECALL RECORDS

Complete chronological transcript with tagged speaker roles and latency markers.

05

Evidence Isolated & Extracted

WHAT HAPPENS

The extractor analyzes the conversation transcript, using ONLY the recipient's speech to parse delivery window, name, and confirmation code.

WHY IT MATTERS

Guarantees zero evidence hallucination. Agent turns and input metadata are strictly forbidden from being counted as proof.

WHAT RESOLVECALL RECORDS

EVIDENCE_EXTRACTED with structured fields and verbatim verbatim conversational quotes.

06

Deterministic Policy Evaluated

WHAT HAPPENS

The policy engine mathematically computes whether the promised window finishes prior to the cutoff deadline.

WHY IT MATTERS

An AI promise is meaningless without deterministic verification. If window > deadline, status = INVALID.

WHAT RESOLVECALL RECORDS

POLICY_EVALUATED with decision (VALID / INVALID / AMBIGUOUS) and exact minute margin.

07

Recovery Recorded & Confirmed

WHAT HAPPENS

The incident transitions to RECOVERED or RECOVERY_UNCONFIRMED based solely on verified policy outcome.

WHY IT MATTERS

Enterprise stakeholders receive guaranteed clarity: either the shipment is saved with proof, or flagged for immediate escalation.

WHAT RESOLVECALL RECORDS

Final state locked into immutable audit trail; downstream systems notified via SSE.

GOVERNANCE & TRUST

ENTERPRISE TELEPHONY SECURITY

Autonomous phone recovery requires strict access controls, zero secret leakage, and deterministic decision boundaries.

🔒

Authorized Phone Destinations

Calls are strictly routed only to phone numbers pre-registered and authorized within the incident schema. Dialpads are strictly locked to prevent arbitrary dialing.

🛡️

Secret & Key Isolation

CALL-E bearer tokens, telephony provider credentials, and access keys reside exclusively in the server environment. The frontend never receives private credentials.

📐

Deterministic Policy Validation

LLMs cannot declare an incident recovered. Recovery is determined solely by mathematical policy evaluation verifying that agreed windows meet deadline constraints.

📜

Immutable Auditability

Every turn, dialed number, status transition, and policy verdict is recorded in an append-only operational audit log suitable for enterprise compliance.

🔍

Conversational Evidence Isolation

To prevent hallucinated recoveries, agent speech is completely excluded from extraction. Only the human operational contact's words count as proof.

⚡

Safe Failure Modes

If a call disconnects, drops, or yields ambiguous information, ResolveCall defaults safely to RECOVERY_UNCONFIRMED, never falsely claiming success.
